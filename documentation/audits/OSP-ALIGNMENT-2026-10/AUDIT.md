# Trazo OSP alignment audit — October 2026

Review dates: September 29–30, 2026. Requested October filename retained. **Internal; evidence-limited review, not certifier acceptance or a completed live regression sign-off.**

## 1. Executive verdict

**CCOF — useful preparation, not a form-complete substitute.** The packet provides substantial crop-management narrative, parcel summaries and supporting workbooks. However, its “forms generated” list overstates what those documents contain. The one-page certification-contract module is not CCOF’s six-page agreement; equipment-cleaning grids, ownership during off-farm handling/storage, some labeling answers and attachments are missing. A reviewer would need supplementary answers and documents, and the agent’s applicable agreements. Some existing clients may already have these on file; their absence from this packet is not proof that every renewing grower must refile them. Trazo cannot currently promise that this packet alone avoids re-entry into CCOF forms.

**WSDA — substantial overlap, incomplete question coverage.** The coverage page maps AGR numbers to Trazo documents, but the module PDFs retain Trazo/CCOF-style numbering. Several questions have no equivalent answer: for example, 2121-O question 5 asks for record location, whereas its “covered” mapping points to record categories. AGR 2196’s restriction-compliance information and AGR 2264’s dated product history are incomplete. AGR 2516 is listed as generated even though the accompanying annual-update page is an explicit non-filing warning. WSDA permits alternative records for some tables, including 2196 and 2516, when the necessary information is present; matching the visual form is therefore less important than supplying every applicable field.

**OTCO — no independent conformance sign-off.** The packet, workbooks and all 283 supplied register rows were available. OTCO’s official form listing and renewal instructions were accessible, but the original DOCX forms and crop ZIP could not be retrieved successfully in this environment. The register is precisely what this audit must independently check; it cannot serve as the official source. Packet-level deficiencies are observable, including the unfiled v4 annual update, missing underlying evidence and unchanged module numbering. A complete official-question verdict for OTCO remains blocked until the original forms are supplied. The appendix preserves every register claim as unverified rather than turning it into an audit pass.

**OCI and MOSA — a general supporting packet only.** A disclaimer is useful, but “only the numbering differs” is not supported. OCI’s actual annual-update form has its own identity, scope, sales and declaration workflow. MOSA publishes a distinct Farm OSP and directs applicants to its process; its allowance for understandable alternative recordkeeping is not blanket acceptance of a CCOF-numbered OSP. Both fallback packets contain instructions to file some forms with CCOF. Replace those directions and label the output as unmapped preparation material. Do not imply acceptance by either agent.

**What to do now:** fix misleading completeness and mapping claims first; obtain an independently verified OTCO source set; demonstrate a completed annual-renewal fixture; then have one crop reviewer assess one corrected packet. Do not commission another broad feature build before that narrow acceptance exercise.

## 2. Method and evidence boundaries

**Observed** means directly present in a supplied file, inspected product surface or retrieved official source. **Inference** means interpretation, including what a reviewer would likely request. **Recommendation** means proposed behavior. No finding asserts that fictional farm records prove a real operation compliant or noncompliant.

The five supplied packets identify OSP v4 and generation on September 29. The builder states that they were generated by production commit `4959284` against a **local copy** of the Calapooia Bend demo, with temporary certifier selection. That provenance is a builder statement, not a repository or deployment verification. Live counts and local fixture counts are kept separate. The earlier Cascade Ridge specimen is not the evidence for this run’s Calapooia conclusions.

Work performed: official CCOF and WSDA source retrieval and reading; comparison with PDF text and workbooks; cross-packet module comparison; review of the register’s mappings and scope; sample quantity and document tracing; selected PDF rendering; official renewal/fallback research; public-copy review; and the earlier partial authenticated inspection. Form tables below are the independent assessment for the supplied specimen, **not a universal test of every conditional branch supported by the code**.

The authenticated inspection reached the OSP, agent picker, completion information and a packet download. Later `api.trazo.io` access was blocked by browser policy. This is **not a Trazo outage**, and no alternative access path to that API was attempted. Per the user’s direction, the supplied files became the evidence for section 5. Consumer QR behavior, mobile workflows, review-link security and WhatsApp could not be finished live. No new TEST objects, links, messages, season finalizations or subscription changes were made during this second run.

OTCO original downloads returned access/format failures. Its official listing does not prove the contents or current revision of the linked files. This report therefore **does not fulfill the requested complete independent OTCO question comparison**. That limitation is explicit and remains a release gate. No coverage percentage is calculated from register rows: they mix questions, grouped subquestions, grids and internal routing statements.

### What “answered” means in the tables

An answer to a question about a **procedure** may be complete without attaching each historical instance of that procedure. A question that explicitly requires an attachment is not complete merely because the operator says it is “on file.” A dated, explicit “no” can validly skip conditional questions. Optional blank spaces are not compliance failures. A missing answer does not prove the application lacks the capability to collect it.

“Not answered, NOT disclosed” means the question is not specifically accounted for on the **coverage page**; a local draft or missing-field warning may still exist. Disclosure fixes honesty, not substantive completeness. A grower must still supply an applicable answer before relying on the packet for intake. Existing certified sites and new sites must follow different routing; do not force all existing parcels through a new-application history workflow each year.

### Partial answers and evidence weaknesses

**Materials.** The planned list and logged-use list are usefully distinct. But the 20-row materials workbook contains blank use-state/comment fields and generic substances alongside brands. The plan refers to a Neptune formulation that differs from the logged formulation; this cannot be resolved by fuzzy name matching. G6.4 mentions Tsunami 100, while warnings refer to SaniDate 5.0 and generic peracetic acid. Preserve those as unresolved identities until the operator selects the exact product and label. WSDA 2196 (R 01/21) additionally asks how restrictions are met. An ingredient’s National List classification or a product’s listing is not a conclusion that this particular use complies. The packet’s own handling-use warning is useful; the “all inputs listed” answer should not conceal unresolved rows.

**Parcels.** All six show multi-year history, manager dates and boundary descriptions. Five have schematic maps with local incompleteness warnings; Home Field East lacks a field map and an address yet shows all five application answers complete in the supplied local fixture. “Eligible” appears beside “none recorded” for prohibited inputs and an unanswered materials declaration. Absence of logged history is weaker evidence than an attested history covering the period, and neither is an agent certification decision. CCOF Parcel Application NOPB49 V1 R11, March 14, 2022, F–G, and WSDA AGR 2262/2264 provide the relevant routing. Do not demand prior-manager declarations when the current manager already covers the period or when the applicable currently-certified branch permits different evidence.

**Attachments.** The ZIPs contain generated records and workbooks, not the underlying invoice/BOL/packout/label/certificate/map evidence needed for the sampled reconstruction. This does not prove those records do not exist elsewhere. A packet manifest should state: embedded; linked and retrievable; held separately with location; missing; or not applicable. A reference number is not an embedded document.

**Contradictions.** Some narratives describe an all-organic operation while G6.0 describes conventional disposition of buffer crops. The on-farm shed may genuinely handle only organic crop; that narrower statement should not become a farm-wide assertion. The stock/transplant narrative calls onion sets annual seedlings; classification should follow the actual purchased material, not a crop-name default. Both are specimen issues to review with the operator, not grounds for automatically changing their answers.

## 8. WhatsApp field notes

**Design verdict:** draft note → reviewed structured record is defensible for suitable record types. The claim that NOP requires the grower personally to author every record is too strong. [7 CFR 205.201(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-I/subchapter-M/part-205/subpart-C/section-205.201) requires an agreed operation plan, and [205.103(b)–(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-I/subchapter-M/part-205/subpart-B/section-205.103) concerns adequate, auditable, retained and accessible records. Preserve who performed the work, who sent the note, who entered/corrected the record and who reviewed it. “Authored by grower” must not overwrite the actual performer.

[21 CFR 112.161(a)(1), (a)(2), (a)(4) and (b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-112/subpart-O/section-112.161), retrieved September 30, has additional requirements for records required under the Produce Safety Rule: contemporaneous creation and performer/observer dating and signature/initials, with supervisory review for specified categories. A later supervisor’s Apply click cannot automatically replace those requirements. Determine whether the record is in scope before presenting a food-safety compliance claim.

The WhatsApp exclusion rationale also needs precision. [WSDA recordkeeping guidance](https://agr.wa.gov/departments/pesticides-and-fertilizers/pesticides/compliance/recordkeeping) and [WAC 16-228-1320](https://apps.leg.wa.gov/Wac/default.aspx?cite=16-228-1320) require seven-year records for covered pesticide applications; they do not establish that every “chemical” action always requires a licensed applicator. Fertilizer, restricted-use pesticide, general-use pesticide, location and performer circumstances must not be conflated. Keeping this channel from creating pesticide-application records can be a sensible product boundary, but the message should route to the proper workflow rather than disappear.

Audio deletion is not inherently prohibited by NOP. The issue is which retained record the operation relies on. If audio is destroyed immediately after confirmation, retain an adequate authoritative transcript/structured record with source identifiers, original wording, corrections, event time and receipt/entry/review times. Do not claim retention compliance without testing that policy, access and export. A missing EXIF tag likewise does not automatically disqualify a photograph as evidence; provenance can come from other linked records and attestations. Restricting photo intake is a product choice, not a universal legal rule.

## 12. Smallest useful real-reviewer exercise

After the first fixes, send one corrected, clearly marked specimen to a willing crop reviewer yourself. Ask only:

1. **Would you use this packet for your intake?** Mark the exact remaining questions you need and which agent forms must still be signed/filed separately.
2. **Is this completed annual-update example sufficient for an existing crop client?** Identify missing changes, site identifiers, sales/agreements or attachments; distinguish initial application from renewal.
3. **Can you reconstruct this one sale and this one actual input application from the included source documents?** Mark the first broken link, not a general opinion about “audit readiness.”
4. **For this crew-note example, whose signature/initials and what retained source would you expect for this record type?** Show original note, proposed fields, confirmation, performer attribution and correction history.

Measure reviewer follow-up requests, duplicate entry, grower time to resolve them, and whether the corrected resubmission closes them. Acceptance by one reviewer for one crop operation is evidence for that scope, not all agents or all production systems. No certifier or company was contacted for this audit.
