# OTCO desk read — renewal packet, 2026-09-10

**Method.** The full renewal packet was generated for Calapooia Bend Farm (Linn County OR; six
parcels; blueberries, carrots, spinach, onions; all 19 modules answered) with Oregon Tilth set as
its certifying agent, and read as an OTCO renewal reviewer would: against the OSP Update Form,
C2, C7, C8, C11, C12 and FACT-04. The farm's agent of record in the dev data is a fictional
certifier, swapped only at generation, so operator prose that still names that agent is not a
finding.

**Result.** The register said this packet covers every OTCO form. A reviewer would still have sent
it back. Five of the reasons were Trazo defects and are fixed in the same change as this file.
The rest are listed below in the order a reviewer would raise them.

## B. Trazo gaps a reviewer would raise, not fixed here

Ranked by how likely each is to produce a request for information.

1. **Post-harvest sanitizer cited to § 205.601.** The flume sanitizer prints as "Peracetic acid —
   § 205.601". Product-contact wash water is a § 205.605 use. The National List catalog holds no
   § 205.605 rows. Verify the current text on eCFR before seeding.
   **Fixed 2026-09-10:** the § 205.605(b) sanitizers are seeded from the eCFR text, the search
   offers both sections for a name listed on both, and the materials page names a logged
   wash-water sanitizer that the plan covers only under § 205.601.
2. **Traced lot does not balance.** Part B of the exhibit lists harvests and sales for one lot but
   never subtracts. An inspector will: the cooler received more fresh fruit than was sold fresh,
   and the page does not say where the difference went.
   **Fixed 2026-09-10:** Part B step 4 states harvested minus sold for the lot, or why it cannot.
3. **No label copies.** G7.0 says consumer packs carry the USDA seal and the certifier statement.
   C8 asks for the labels. The packet has no way to carry them.
4. **Handler certificates not attached.** G6.1 names two off-farm handlers and says, correctly,
   "A copy of the certificate is not embedded". Uploaded facility documents do not reach the
   packet's attachment loop.
5. **Non-organic seed is not itemized.** G3.0 says non-organic untreated seed is used and searches
   are documented. No page lists which lots were non-organic or which searches cover them, though
   the seed records hold both.
6. **Materials reconcile by exact name only.** Plan rows carry catalog names; logs carry trade
   names. All ten logged products print as unmatched, and the Materials List workbook shows each
   product twice. The logged events on this farm carry no OMRI or WSDA link to reconcile by.
7. **Last prohibited input is blank on every row** of the Field and Crop form, beside parcel pages
   that say none is recorded and an operator attestation of no prohibited use in three years.
8. **Plan snapshot header prints "Certifier Template: —".**
