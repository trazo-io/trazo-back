# WSDA desk read — renewal packet, 2026-09-10

**Method.** The full renewal packet was generated for Cascade Ridge Orchards (Naches WA; three
apple blocks, 59.4 acres; plan v1 submitted, all 19 modules answered) and read as a WSDA renewal
reviewer would: against AGR 2121 sections A–R, the AGR 2196 Input Material List, the AGR 2264
Site Application and the AGR 2516 Site Update. Unlike the OTCO read, no agent was swapped: the
certificate of record names the WSDA Organic Program, and the packet resolved it unaided. The
farm is dev specimen data and every page carries the SPECIMEN mark.

**Result.** Resolution and numbering worked. The coverage page used WSDA's form numbers, named the
four workbooks, and listed WSDA's handler-scope forms as not generated. A reviewer would still have
sent the packet back. Seven of the reasons were Trazo defects and are fixed in the same change as
this file. The rest are listed below in the order a reviewer would raise them.

## B. Trazo gaps a reviewer would raise, not fixed here

Ranked by how likely each is to produce a request for information.

1. **Contradictions Trazo could catch from its own records, but prints without comment.**
   - The Materials Application says a prohibited material was used on this land in the last three years. Every parcel page says no prohibited application is recorded, with status Eligible.
   - G4.1 says raw manure goes only on land growing crops not intended for human consumption. The operation grows only apples.
   - G4.0's erosion answer ticks two practices together with "Not applicable — no erosion risk". The API refuses that combination on save and on completion (`exclusive_conflicts`), so real data should not reach it. The PDF still prints it if it is stored.
2. **Four § 205.601 catalogue rows are not on § 205.601.** Checked against the eCFR text: *Microbial
   products (Bacillus thuringiensis, etc.)*, *Spinosad* and two *d-Limonene* rows are filed at
   § 205.601(e), and none is in that section. Bt and spinosad are nonsynthetic, so they are allowed
   without a listing. The d-limonene notes cite an NOSB recommendation, and the spinosad note cites an
   EPA label; neither is the rule. Here, Entrust SC resolves to the spinosad row, so anything that
   prints the row's section tells a reviewer the product is a listed synthetic. This change stops the
   Materials List quoting any § 205.601 note. The rows themselves need re-filing as nonsynthetic,
   through a migration that keeps the plans and events attached to them.
3. **Handler certificate not attached.** Bins go to Stemilt within 24 hours and are held in Stemilt's controlled-atmosphere rooms under Stemilt's certificate. G6.2 says the operation holds a copy of that certificate, but the packet carries none (OTCO read B4).
4. **Materials have no maker and no listing link.** No logged event carries an OMRI or WSDA link, so Manufacturer is blank for six of the seven products and the Supplier List's certifier column is blank throughout (OTCO read B6). A WSDA reviewer checks each product against the WSDA Brand Name Materials List.
5. **Farm equipment (AGR 2121 §I, 10 request-for-information rows in the register).** Cleaning of shared or custom equipment is answered only in free text inside G6.0 and G6.1.
6. **Lot-numbering claim.** G8.0 answers "Yes — Trazo lot IDs", which asserts a numbering system Trazo does not run: `lot_id` is free text (register rows OTCO 12.2-3 and WSDA §O 8).
7. **Two crop-label formats on one form.** The Field and Crop Information Form lists "Gala apples", taken from land history, beside "Apples (2026 season)", taken from an open season that records no variety.
8. **Last prohibited input is blank** on every row of the Field and Crop Information Form (OTCO read B7).
9. **A stored "no" can print as Yes.** The PDF renders a yes/no answer as `"Yes" if value else "No"`, and
   conditions test truthiness the same way, so the STRING "no" prints Yes and opens the follow-ups
   that belong to Yes. The write boundary refuses strings (`invalid_typed_answers`), and the dev
   database holds them only on two specimen plans seeded around the API. The renderer still trusts
   its input; a stored answer that is not true/false should be flagged, never inverted.

## C. Items only the operator can answer

- The annual update says "45.2 acres across Ridge Block and Creek Block". The records hold a third block, Bench Block: 14.2 acres of Cripps Pink, organic in every year of its land history (2023–2025). The cover lists it under "Parcels this plan never names"; no change notification names it.
- G8.0 §A has no record types confirmed, so Part A of the audit trail is empty.
- The traced lot CRO-2025-RB harvested 971,100 lb and sold 941,967 lb. The page asks where the other 29,133 lb went.
- Calcium chloride is logged as a ground spray. The § 205.602(c) use is a foliar spray for a calcium-uptake disorder. The operator should state the purpose (bitter pit) and the method.
- Five sections are unfinished: G4.1, G6.0, G6.1, G6.4 and G8.0.
- Several checkbox answers contradict the explanation written beside them:
  - G6.4 ticks washing, hulling and drying, then says nothing is handled on the ranch.
  - G7.0 ticks consumer packaging and three label categories, then says the crop leaves as unlabelled bulk.
  - G3.0b says all planting stock is certified organic, then that it is non-organic and untreated.
  - G8.0 ticks import, export and "neither".
- Fifteen answers on seven module pages read "Recorded in Trazo against this block and reviewed at the January plan review." That is a placeholder, not an answer. It stands in for the compost C:N ratio, temperatures and turnings, the timing of raw manure, and the export countries, among others.

## D. Before this specimen goes to an outside reader

Most of section C comes from this specimen's data, not from the product. The checkbox answers were
seeded separately from the explanations beside them, and 33 yes/no answers were stored as the
strings "yes" and "no", which the API refuses. Every stored "no" printed Yes: G5.0's "crop residue
is burned" and G6.2's "storage shared with nonorganic product" are the operator saying No. A WSDA-experienced reader given this packet
would spend the read on those contradictions instead of on the product. Before it is sent, the
specimen needs a pass that makes every checkbox agree with its explanation, replaces the
placeholder text, and names Bench Block in the annual update.
