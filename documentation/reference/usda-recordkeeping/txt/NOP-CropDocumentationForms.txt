  (Date)


  (Name and address)




  Dear (Name):
  I am currently a certiﬁed organic farmer with ____________________ (name of your certifying agent), managing
  my ﬁelds in a manner consistent with the USDA National Organic Program regulations.
  Since you are an adjoining property owner, I need to inform you of my plans and ask for your help. If you plan to
  use synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, and/or genetically engineered crops on land that adjoins my ﬁelds, please take
  precautions when transporting or spraying to prevent over spray, chemical or genetic drift, or run-oﬀ onto my farm.
  If chemical drift is found on my organic crops or ﬁelds, I may be required to wait up to three years before using
  these ﬁelds for organic production. This could also cause loss of my organic certiﬁcation and/or loss of the organic
  premium for crops grown on aﬀected ﬁelds.
