## 🎯 **Agricultural Spanish Terms Used**

### **Technical Terminology**
- **"Manejo Integrado de Plagas" (MIP)** → Standard term for IPM in Latin America
- **"Especies Benéficas"** → Beneficial species (natural enemies)
- **"Liberación de Benéficos"** → Biocontrol release terminology
- **"Umbral Económico"** → Economic threshold (standard pest management term)
- **"Nivel de Presión"** → Pressure level (commonly used in agricultural contexts)

### **Regional Considerations (es-419)**
- **"Monitoreo"** → Used instead of "Exploración" for scouting (more common in Latin America)
- **"Trampas"** → Standard term for monitoring traps
- **"Especies de Plagas"** → Clear identification of pest species
- **"Cantidad Liberada"** → Specific to biocontrol release quantities
